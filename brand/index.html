Colour

Each tile has one colour, chosen from the material the name comes from. The glyph on a tile is always the same near-white, and the mono glyph takes whatever colour the text around it has.

Construction and use

Every glyph sits on a 64-unit square inside an 8-unit margin, drawn with a 4.5-unit stroke, round caps and round joins. That is what keeps the family reading as one hand at 16 pixels, where the stroke is about one device pixel.

  • +The tile stays square and stays its own colour. Platforms that round avatars will round it for you.
  • +Use the tile version on any ground. Use the mono version inline, where it inherits the text colour, or wherever a single-colour mark is required.
  • +Do not draw a mark smaller than 16 pixels, and do not put the glyph on a tile of another product's colour.
  • +The dot in the wordmark is always the accent. In running text the name is written EnrichMeAI.

The company that owns the trading name has a mark of its own, a shepherd's crook on pasture green, drawn on the same grid. It lives on goodshepherdconsultancy.com.
